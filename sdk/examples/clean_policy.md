# Vendor Risk Review — Q3 2026

## Methodology

We assessed twelve vendors using a weighted scorecard across security,
reliability, and cost. Scores derive from SOC 2 reports dated within the
last 14 months, reference calls with two existing customers per vendor,
and a 40-item technical questionnaire. Weights: security 50%,
reliability 30%, cost 20%.

## Findings

Based on the scorecard, three vendors meet our bar. Acme Cloud scored
highest at 87/100. According to our reference calls, their incident
response times averaged under 30 minutes in 2025. These results suggest
Acme is likely the strongest fit, though pricing may vary by region.

## Limitations

This review covers only the twelve vendors in scope and does not assess
subprocessors beyond tier one. Scores reflect point-in-time evidence and
may change as vendors update their controls. We did not perform
penetration testing. Cost estimates are approximate and subject to
negotiation.
