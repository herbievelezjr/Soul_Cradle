# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **MytharaLabsLLC@yahoo.com** with subject `SECURITY: Soul Cradle`.
Include what you found, what you tested it against, and how to reproduce it.
We will acknowledge within 5 business days.

Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability until we
have had a chance to address it.

## Scope

This policy covers the `soul_cradle/` framework and the `sdk/` document
evaluator in this repository. The honest contracts apply here too: report
what you observed — we will verify before we claim anything.

## What is out of scope

Social-engineering reports, theoretical cryptographic breaks without a
working demonstration against this code, and issues in third-party
dependencies (report those upstream).
